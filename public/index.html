07 / Our origins

Born from a question about accountability.

Funded Destruction began as a collaborative project by five people from four countries who met at the 2026 Alternative Economic and Monetary Systems (AEMS) summer school in Vienna. Our original purpose remains at the center: to expose the systemic decisions behind serious environmental destruction and advocate for meaningful accountability, including legal recognition of ecocide.

The platform is being developed beyond its original academic-project format. Investigations, submissions and research products will follow a clear editorial and verification process.
