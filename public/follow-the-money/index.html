What counts as a verified connection?

Direct transaction

Documented payment, donation, grant, or investment, attributed to the correct entity.

Disclosed association

Membership or corporate relationship supported by public documents, without assuming a particular payment.

Reported allegation

Clearly attributed reporting or allegation, separate from a legal finding or proven transaction.

Publication safeguards

  • Match every record to the correct legal entity, subsidiary, reporting period, and jurisdiction.
  • Publish the original source URL and retrieval or verification date.
  • Distinguish spending, donations, lobbying, investments, and memberships.
  • Separate allegations, regulatory actions, settlements, and final legal findings.
  • Provide a correction and challenge process for published information.
  • Do not expose sensitive evidence or contributor identity through public records.
